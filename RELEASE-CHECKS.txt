Rendering architecture:
- Geometry calculations run in assets/workers/geometry-baker.js.
- Geometry is partitioned into 16x16x16 chunks.
- Meshes use indexed BufferGeometry and Uint16/Uint32 index buffers.
- Large layers (>= 1800 blocks) receive 1x/2x/4x/8x distance LOD.
- Single-block edits rebuild target chunks and face-adjacent boundary chunks.
- A per-layer chunk index avoids scanning every block during a local edit.
- Special model shape construction remains on the main thread, then its triangles are compiled by the Worker.

Limitations:
- Real-browser end-to-end regression with large user structures could not be completed in this environment.
- Coarse voxel LOD is approximate; it may fill small holes or alter sparse silhouettes.
- Special-model geometry is retained at full detail in coarse LODs, so special-heavy structures gain less LOD reduction.
- Actual performance varies with structure density, material count, GPU, and browser. No real-device FPS claim is made.
- BDX support is partial and import-only.
- MCA support is partial and import-only.
- MCWorld support extracts supported embedded structure files; LevelDB world database read/write is not implemented.
- NBT is import-only.
