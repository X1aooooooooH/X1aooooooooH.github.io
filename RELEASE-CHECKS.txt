BlockFlow v2.11.1 — MCStructure-only, detailed redstone geometry, Worker/Chunk/LOD

Scope:
- Structure Editor imports and exports Minecraft Bedrock .mcstructure only.
- Alternate-format adapters, converters, import/export selectors and converter workers are removed.
- Sign boards/text, regular/redstone/soul/copper torches, levers, buttons, repeaters, comparators and redstone dust use dedicated 3D geometry builders.
- Sign front/back text is read from block-entity NBT and generated as indexed, extruded voxel-like geometry (not a flat text texture).
- Geometry baking runs in a Worker, with indexed geometry, 16^3 chunks, LOD and partial chunk rebuilding.

Automated validation:
- Native baker: 22 checks passed.
- Geometry Worker: 9 checks passed.
- Atlas map: 4 checks passed.
- MCStructure-only and detailed-model suite: 5 test groups passed (45 assertions).
- Five MCStructure/model test groups (45 assertions): passed.
- Three existing suites: baker 22/22, geometry Worker 9/9, atlas-map 4/4.
- JavaScript syntax checks: passed.
- ZIP integrity: checked after packaging.

Known limits:
- Detailed-model regression checks verify source paths and geometry construction; broad browser screenshots and all Minecraft block-state variants still need visual regression.
- Sign text glyphs are rasterized into voxel-like geometry; font appearance can vary by platform.
- Coarse voxel LOD is approximate and may alter small holes or sparse silhouettes.
- Special-model source geometry is prepared on the main thread before Worker compilation.
- Actual frame rate varies with structure, material count, GPU and browser; no device FPS claim is made.
